Old European Boutique Chateaus and each with a Private Screened in Gazebo, Walkout Balcony and Outdoor Hot Tub to soothe life’s pains away while enjoying views of spectacular scenery overlooking sparkling G aleairy Lake onto the romantic forests of Algonquin Park.
